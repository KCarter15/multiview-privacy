YouTube TV Multiview Privacy Policy

This policy applies to version 0.1.0 of YouTube TV Multiview, an independent Chrome extension that arranges YouTube TV pages into a two- or four-screen grid and lets you choose which screen supplies audio.

Information handled on your device

The extension stores your most recent YouTube TV page URL for each pane in your Chrome profile so it can reopen those pages. It also saves your layout, selected audio pane, and keyboard shortcut settings. A temporary tab identifier helps the extension find and manage its open grid during the browser session.

Inside the grid, the extension accesses YouTube TV page URLs and media elements to track navigation and control audio. It responds to configured keyboard shortcuts and pointer movement, which reveals the fullscreen controls. The extension keeps no keystroke log, pointer history, audio recording, or video recording, and does not read or store your Google password or authentication cookies.

Information sent over the network

The embedded YouTube TV pages connect directly to Google as part of ordinary navigation and playback. When the extension reopens a saved pane, Chrome requests its saved URL from YouTube TV. YouTube TV may use your existing sign-in and handle playback, account, and location information under Google’s Privacy Policy.

The extension has no developer-operated backend, analytics, or advertising trackers. It does not send saved settings or activity records to the developer, sell user data, or use it for advertising, creditworthiness, or lending.

Permissions

The extension uses Chrome storage for local settings and temporary grid state. It requests access to tv.youtube.com to work with the embedded players. A temporary Chrome request rule removes the X-Frame-Options and Content-Security-Policy headers from matching YouTube TV frame responses in the grid tab to enable embedding.

Retention and deletion

The Chrome profile keeps settings and the most recent page URL for each pane until the values are replaced or you remove the extension. To delete the extension’s saved data, open Chrome’s Extensions page and uninstall YouTube TV Multiview.

Limited Use and contact

YouTube TV Multiview’s handling of user data adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. For questions about this policy, use the developer contact or support information on the extension’s Chrome Web Store listing.
